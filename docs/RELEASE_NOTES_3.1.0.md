# MacEdgeLight 3.1.0

Adjustable display brightness boost strength.

- Adds a **Boost Strength** menu (10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) under the menu bar icon. The choice is saved and applies immediately while boost is on.
- Strength is applied against each display's safe ceiling, so the whole range stays usable on displays with little headroom. 100% matches 3.0.4.
- Shows a CONFLICT state in the menu and toolbar when another app appears to keep rewriting the display gamma table. Boost keeps running; turn it off to stop the competition.
- **Reset to Defaults** restores 100%. Ring-light reset does not change strength.

Gamma safety clamps, ColorSync restoration, recovery timing, and the 30-second rapid-toggle waiting interval are unchanged.
