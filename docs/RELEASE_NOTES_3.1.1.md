# MacEdgeLight 3.1.1

Brighter display brightness boost.

- Raises the boost gamma maximum from 1.55 to 2.4 and removes the 90%-of-live-headroom clamp. The neutral 1.0 floor remains.
- Boost Strength levels now scale toward the 2.4 maximum, so 100% is brighter than in 3.1.0.
- Highlights can clip when macOS grants less headroom than the ramp expects. Turn boost off or lower Boost Strength if that happens.

ColorSync restoration, recovery timing, and the 30-second rapid-toggle waiting interval are unchanged.
